Job Title: Senior iOS Developer
Company: Apex Mobile Inc.
Location: San Francisco, CA (Hybrid)
Experience: 5+ years

About the Role:
We are looking for a Senior iOS Developer to join our growing mobile team. You will be responsible for architecting and building high-quality iOS applications used by millions of users worldwide.

Responsibilities:
- Design and implement new features for our core iOS applications
- Lead code reviews and mentor junior developers
- Collaborate with product managers, designers, and backend engineers
- Architect scalable, maintainable, and testable code
- Optimize app performance and ensure smooth UX
- Publish and manage apps through the App Store
- Stay up to date with the latest iOS SDK, Swift, and Apple platform changes

Requirements:
- 5+ years of professional iOS development experience
- Strong proficiency in Swift; Objective-C experience is a plus
- Deep knowledge of UIKit, SwiftUI, and Auto Layout
- Experience with RESTful APIs and JSON parsing
- Familiarity with MVVM, MVC, or VIPER architecture patterns
- Strong understanding of memory management, threading, and concurrency (GCD, async/await)
- Experience with Core Data, Realm, or other local persistence solutions
- Proficiency with Xcode, Instruments, and debugging tools
- Experience with CI/CD pipelines (Fastlane, GitHub Actions, Bitrise)
- Solid understanding of App Store guidelines and submission process
- Experience writing unit and UI tests (XCTest, XCUITest)

Nice to Have:
- Experience with Swift Package Manager and modular architecture
- Familiarity with GraphQL or WebSockets
- Experience with push notifications, deep linking, and background processing
- Prior experience with AR (ARKit) or ML (Core ML) features
- Open source contributions

Compensation:
- Base salary: $160,000 – $210,000
- Equity package
- Health, dental, vision benefits
- Annual learning & development budget
