Job Title: Junior Data Scientist
Department: Data & Analytics
Location: Remote (US)
Employment Type: Full-Time

About the Role
We are looking for a curious and motivated Junior Data Scientist to join our growing Data & Analytics team. You will work closely with senior data scientists and business stakeholders to build models, analyze data, and surface insights that drive product and business decisions.

This is a great opportunity for someone early in their career who wants to work across the full data science lifecycle — from data wrangling and exploration to model building and deployment.

Responsibilities
- Clean, transform, and explore large datasets to identify patterns and trends
- Build, evaluate, and iterate on machine learning models for classification, regression, and clustering tasks
- Collaborate with data engineers to access and prepare data from various sources
- Communicate findings clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders
- Write clean, well-documented Python code and maintain reproducible notebooks
- Assist in deploying and monitoring models in production

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or a related quantitative field
- 0–2 years of experience in data science or a related role (internships count)
- Proficiency in Python and core data science libraries: pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn
- Familiarity with supervised and unsupervised machine learning algorithms
- Understanding of statistics: hypothesis testing, distributions, regression
- Experience with data visualization tools (matplotlib, seaborn, or similar)
- Comfortable working with SQL for data extraction and aggregation
- Familiarity with Jupyter Notebooks or similar environments

Nice to Have
- Experience with deep learning frameworks (PyTorch or TensorFlow)
- Exposure to MLflow, DVC, or other experiment tracking tools
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- Contributions to open source or Kaggle competition experience

What We Offer
- Competitive salary and equity
- Mentorship from senior data scientists
- Access to modern data infrastructure and tooling
- Learning & development budget
