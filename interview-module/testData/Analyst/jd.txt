Job Title: Business / Data Analyst
Department: Strategy & Analytics
Location: San Jose, CA (Hybrid)
Employment Type: Full-Time

About the Role
We are looking for a detail-oriented and analytically driven Business Analyst to join our Strategy & Analytics team. You will work with stakeholders across marketing, finance, and operations to transform data into insights that inform business decisions.

This role is a great fit for someone with a strong background in research reporting and data automation who is looking to grow their technical skills in modern data tooling.

Responsibilities
- Gather, clean, and analyze data from multiple sources to answer business questions
- Build and maintain automated reports and dashboards for internal stakeholders
- Apply statistical methods to validate findings and surface actionable insights
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define KPIs and measurement frameworks
- Document processes, methodologies, and data definitions clearly
- Present findings to both technical and non-technical audiences

Requirements
- 4+ years of experience in a data, research, or analytics role
- Strong proficiency in Excel (including VBA/macros) and PowerPoint
- Experience with statistical analysis methods: t-tests, correlation, regression
- Familiarity with SPSS, SQL, or similar data tools
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills
- Bachelor's degree in a quantitative field (Computer Science, Statistics, Business, or related)

Nice to Have
- Experience with Python (pandas, NumPy) or R for data analysis
- Exposure to BI tools (Tableau, Power BI, Looker)
- Graduate degree in a technical field
- Experience in market research or survey analytics
